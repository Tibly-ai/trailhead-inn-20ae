Lodge Intelligence

Stay Guidelines

Check-In and Out Times

Arrival is welcome from 3:00 PM onwards. Self-check-in codes are issued the morning of arrival. We ask that guests check out by 11:00 AM so we can thoroughly prep the spaces for the next travelers.

Wildlife and Surroundings

The lodge borders protected wild meadows. Deer, elk, and wild birds move through the grounds daily. We expect guests to observe from a distance and secure food waste in designated airtight cabin bins.

Connectivity & Quiet Hours

Each room has access to quiet Starlink wifi, perfect for focused remote work. To preserve the wilderness soundtrack, outdoor quiet hours begin at 10:00 PM. No external speakers are permitted at any time.

Pet Policy

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in our dedicated Ridge Cabins for a small standard cleaning fee. Please coordinate with us prior to arrival so we can lay out custom dog linens and bowls.
